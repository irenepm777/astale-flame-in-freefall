Downloaded on 14-11-2026 from the official NASA library at https://images.nasa.gov (API images-api.nasa.gov).

## Can they be used?

Yes. Material created by NASA is not subject to copyright in the United States, and it may be used on websites, videos, and presentations (usage guide: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).

Conditions:
- Cite the source: “Credit: NASA” (and the experiment name if desired).
- Do not use the NASA logo (the blue “meatball”) or imply that NASA endorses us. For this reason, the two candle comparisons (GRC-1998-C-00485/00486) have a cropped version without the logo: vela-tierra-vs-espacio.jpg and vela-tierra-vs-espacio-2.jpg.
- Do not use images showing identifiable astronauts in contexts that resemble advertising.
- The file jsc2024e016238 (flame over cable) was discarded: it belongs to Hokkaido University, not NASA. None of the downloaded images carry third-party credit in their official description.
